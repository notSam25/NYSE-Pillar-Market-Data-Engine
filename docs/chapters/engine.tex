\section{Engine}

At its core, the engine should be a decoder and a source of heuristics. This
abstracts away much of the complexity involved in working with the data,
allowing the engine to relay decoded messages to a consumer as quickly as
possible.\\ Characteristics that the engine could or should track include
\texttt{msg/sec}, gap detection, and the specific messages decoded.\\ Features
that the engine could provide may include viewing specific messages and
replaying a selected interval of messages.
